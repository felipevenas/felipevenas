## About

Fullstack developer with 2 years of experience shipping production software, currently on the Software Development & Innovation team at **Alcance Contabilidade**, building APIs, integrations and workflow automations for real business operations.

Backend-first with **Python**, **FastAPI**, **Celery** and **Redis**; frontend with **React**, **TypeScript** and **Astro**. I use AI agents daily to deliver faster without sacrificing quality. Driven by clean code, security and simplicity. Based in Bahia, Brazil.

## What I care about

- **Clean architecture.** Explicit boundaries, pure domain rules and dependencies that point inward — enforced by tools such as ArchUnit and ESLint, not only by convention.
- **Security by default.** Short-lived sessions, encrypted secrets, strict authorization and LGPD-aware handling of personal data.
- **Developer experience.** Small, predictable setup steps; typed contracts; useful error messages; no hidden magic.
- **Documentation that tells the truth.** Architecture, trade-offs and known limitations belong in the README, next to the code.
